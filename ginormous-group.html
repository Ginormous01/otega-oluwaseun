01 / ABOUT THE GROUP

Different paths.
Shared purpose.

Ginormous Group of Companies brings together four distinct ventures: Ginormous Investments, Ginormous Suites, Ginormous Farms, and Ginormous Children and Teens Foundation.

The group spans property investment and development, homes and properties, farming and agro-real estate, and training and mentorship for young people. Each venture has its own focus while contributing to the wider work of Ginormous Group.
