02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PURPOSE

About
Me.

I am Otega Oluwaseun, a child inspirer, teens mentor, real estate and agro-real estate investor, husband, and father.

I had a very interesting childhood in Ejigbo. My entrepreneurial journey began as early as Primary 3, and since then, I have developed a deep passion for providing solutions and creating value through entrepreneurship.

I have a burning passion for helping children and teenagers develop the right mindset toward life and their desired future. Through training and mentorship, I am committed to helping young people break free from limiting beliefs, take charge of their lives, and build futures they never imagined possible.

As an investor and entrepreneur, I also help people build wealth through real estate and agro-real estate investments, providing access to genuine investment opportunities through Ginormous Group of Companies.
